# Monitor Dashboard Audit

The Monitor Dashboard (`/app/monitor`) serves as the central "Command Center" for AI Search insights. It effectively replaces the legacy Optimization Dashboard.

## Key Sections & Features

### 1. **Pulse & High-Level Metrics**
- **Visibility Score:** The primary KPI. Shows your Share of Voice in AI answers (0-100).
- **Brand Defense:** Tracks how well you dominate searches for *your own brand name*. Critical for protecting against competitors conquesting your terms.
- **Avg. Sentiment:** The overall "vibe" of AI mentions (Positive/Neutral/Negative).

### 2. **Trends & Analysis**
- **Visibility Chart:** A line chart comparing your visibility trend against top competitors over time (7/30/90 days).
- **Brands Table:** A ranked list of all detected brands in your niche, showing their Visibility %, Sentiment, and Rank Position. Includes trend indicators (↑/↓).

### 3. **Deep Dive Insights**
- **Recommendation Rate:** The % of time AI explicitly recommends your product (e.g., "I recommend [Brand] because...").
- **Active Sources:** The number of unique websites (citations) driving your AI visibility.
- **Competitor Gap:** A direct comparison showing how far ahead or behind you are vs. your top rival.

### 4. **Strategic Intelligence**
- **Competitor Intelligence Cards:** Highlights specific "Winning Factors" or snippets where competitors are being praised. This helps you understand *why* they are ranking.
- **Revenue at Risk:** An estimated monetary value of lost traffic/sales due to low visibility (based on a heuristic model).

### 5. **Actionability (The "So What?")**
- **Action Plan:** A dynamic list of "Optimization Opportunities" (e.g., "Fix descriptions for 3 products").
- **Link to Fix Engine:** Directly bridges the gap between **Insight** (Monitor) and **Action** (Improve), allowing users to fix issues immediately.

## Assessment
**Status:** ✅ **Comprehensive & Logical**

The dashboard effectively answers the three core questions of AIO (Artificial Intelligence Optimization):
1.  **"Am I visible?"** (Visibility Score, Trends)
2.  **"Do people like me?"** (Sentiment, Recommendation Rate)
3.  **"How do I win?"** (Competitor Gap, Action Plan)

The data flow is now consistent with the Onboarding results, ensuring a seamless user experience from first install to daily monitoring.
